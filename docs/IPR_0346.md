# Native progressive preview candidate — 0.3.46

Persistent sessions now request RenderMode::PROGRESSIVE rather than BATCH. This applies to persistent previews only; standalone final rendering keeps its existing mode. Normal progressive rendering retains full material shading, rather than the approximate PROGRESSIVE_FAST modes.

Snapshots use readiness and film-activity checks, attempt the first image immediately when ready, then limit successful image polling to approximately 10 Hz. AOV selection can trigger a snapshot without new samples. The render loop checks pending commands every 20 ms. Shared image backpressure remains bounded. A completed short pass attempts to publish an image before writing outputs; a busy shared mailbox can defer that image to the normal completed-output path.

Completed EXRs, display conversion, preparation time, and the cost of scene capture remain. No latency or quality improvement has been measured. The IPR width/sample limits and denoising bypass remain unchanged. Progressive tile traversal is governed by MoonRay's progressive scheduler and scene settings.
